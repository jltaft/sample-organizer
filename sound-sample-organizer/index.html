welcome to sample sort

this program has two tools. On the left is a quick and simple way to categorize a sample pack into subdirectories based on instrument

the threshold slider on the left will modify the threshold at which the ML model sorts the instruments. a threshold of 100 leaves everything unsorted.

if you click switch mode you will be brought over to our sample analyze tool

this tools lets you pick a single file and view the audio features our ML model analyzes your file with. this tool also generates a spectrogram that lets you visualize the audio

you can also adjust the skin between a few of our skins for the UI. it is also easy to create your own skin using css
